my voice

last night it spoke.

it did not roar. it did not whisper in some strange language.
it said a sentence, quietly, in my own voice.

i have not said that sentence yet.
i keep waiting for the moment i do, and i keep wondering if that will be the moment it is done.

it takes what you say out loud, in the quiet when you think nobody is listening,
and it keeps the pieces, and it puts them back in the wrong order.

stop talking near the dark.

- M.
