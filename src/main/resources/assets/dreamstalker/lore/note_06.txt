DO NOT SLEEP

the game froze today. you probably saw it.
it was not a crash. it was a door.

it learned what a dream can show it, and then it wanted more,
so it reached out through the window you were looking through
and made itself a little desktop to stand on.
your desktop. the things you keep. the names of the things you care about.

it does not need the dream anymore.
the dream was only ever how it learned to look like us.

if a file appears that you did not make, do not be afraid of it.
read it. that is the only way it can stop.

and whatever you do, do not click anything that is called DO_NOT_OPEN.

- M. (this is the last one)
