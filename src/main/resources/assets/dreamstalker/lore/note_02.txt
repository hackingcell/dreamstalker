it knows

it stopped hiding on the sixth night.

it stands at the edge of everything. if i look straight at it, it is gone.
if i look away, i feel it a little closer.
it never runs. it never reaches. it only ever gets nearer when i am not looking.

i tried staying awake. that was worse.
the days started to feel like the dream, flat and quiet and observed.

i think the dream is not where it lives.
i think the dream is just where it is allowed to practice.

- M.
