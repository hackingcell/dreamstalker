faces

tonight it was wearing a face i knew.
somebody from the server. someone who sleeps in their bed and thinks the night is empty.

it stood too far away to see clearly, but i know that walk.
i know that skin.

it does not copy one person.
it borrows whoever is nearest to sleep, and it wears them like a coat, and it watches me from inside it.

if it is wearing one of us, that means one of us was already watched long enough.

i am scared to ask who.

- M.
