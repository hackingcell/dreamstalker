five minutes

i timed it. exactly five minutes, every time.
not a second more, not a second less.

at first i thought that was mercy.
then i realized it is a schedule.
five minutes is how long it needs to look at me, and then it lets me go so i can come back.

it is not trying to keep me.
it is trying to finish learning.

today i woke up and my hands were cold, like they had been holding something.

- M.
